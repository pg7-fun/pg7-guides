How to Play Blackjack: Complete Guide [2026]

📅 Published April 9, 2026📝 11 min read👤 PG7 Team
BlackjackCasino21Strategy

Blackjack is the most popular casino table game in the world. The rules are straightforward, the pace is fast, and skilled players can reduce the house edge to less than 1%. This guide covers the fundamentals and introduces basic strategy for new players.

Card Values

  • Number cards (2-10): Face value
  • Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points
  • Ace: 1 point or 11 points — you choose whichever benefits your hand more

Basic Gameplay

  1. Place Your Bet. Before cards are dealt, place your wager in the betting circle in front of your seat.
  2. Receive Your Cards. You and the dealer each receive two cards. Your cards are face up; the dealer's first card is face up, and the second is face down (the 'hole card').
  3. Make Your Decision. Based on your hand total and the dealer's visible card, decide to Hit, Stand, or use one of the advanced options.
  4. Dealer Plays. Once all players have finished, the dealer reveals their hole card and must Hit on 16 or less and Stand on 17 or more.
  5. Settlement. Players who bust lose immediately. Remaining players compare hands to the dealer. Higher hand wins; ties push.

Advanced Options

Double Down

Double your original bet in exchange for receiving exactly one additional card. Ideal when you have a 9, 10, or 11 and the dealer shows a weak upcard.

Split

If your first two cards have the same rank, you can split them into two separate hands, each with its own bet equal to your original wager.

Surrender

In some games, you can forfeit half your bet and end the round before playing your hand. Useful when you have a very weak hand and the dealer shows a strong upcard.

Basic Strategy Reference

When to Hit or Stand

  • Always hit on 8 or less
  • Stand on 17+ (hard total)
  • Stand on 12-16 when dealer shows 2-6; hit when dealer shows 7+
  • Always stand on soft 19 and above
  • Double on 11 (or hit if dealer shows an Ace)
