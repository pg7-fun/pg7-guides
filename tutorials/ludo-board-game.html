How to Play Ludo: Complete Guide [2026]

📅 Published April 9, 2026📝 9 min read👤 PG7 Team
LudoBoard GameFamily GameRules

Ludo — from the Latin word 'I play' — is a classic strategy board game that has been enjoyed by families across India, South Asia, and around the world for generations. The game combines luck (through dice rolls) with tactical decision-making.

Board Layout and Token Colors

A standard Ludo board is divided into four colored quadrants: Red, Blue, Green, and Yellow. Each color has four tokens that start in the home base in their respective corner. Players race their tokens around the board in a clockwise direction to reach the central home column.

Key Board Areas

  • Home Base: The starting area where all four tokens of a color begin. A token must roll a 6 to leave home.
  • Starting Square: The square at the entrance to each player's colored track. Landing here grants safety from captures.
  • Safe Zones: Star-marked squares scattered across the board where tokens cannot be captured.
  • Finish Area: The central column of your color. Only your tokens can enter this column, and exact rolls are needed to move tokens onto the center finish spot.

Gameplay Rules

  1. Roll the Die. Players take turns rolling a single die. The highest roller typically goes first.
  2. Move Tokens. Roll a 6 to move a token from home to the starting square. Roll any number to advance an existing token by that many squares.
  3. Capture. Land directly on an opponent's token to send it back to their home base.
  4. Roll Again on 6. Rolling a 6 earns an extra turn. However, rolling three consecutive 6s in most rule sets forfeits that turn.
  5. Reach Home. Move all four tokens into the finish area to win.

Winning Strategies

Prioritize Safety Early

In the opening phase, focus on getting tokens to safe zones before advancing aggressively. A token captured early costs several turns to redeploy.

Attack When Safe to Do So

When you have tokens on or near safe zone squares, advance aggressively. Opponents landing on your safe square cannot capture you.

Balance Offense and Defense

Distributing tokens across the board rather than clustering them behind one leader creates multiple threats for opponents.

Use Blockades Strategically

Two tokens on the same square form an unbreakable blockade. Blocking an opponent's only exit from home can significantly slow their progress.
