What Is Teen Patti?

Teen Patti — literally 'three cards' in Hindi — is a simplified poker-style card game that has become one of the most widely played games across South Asia and diaspora communities worldwide. Played with a standard 52-card deck, the objective is to hold the best three-card hand and win the pot.

Whether you are playing in a traditional setting or online, understanding the hand rankings and betting structure is essential before you sit down at the table.

Teen Patti is a gambling card game of skill and luck. Always play responsibly and within your means. Check the legality in your region before playing.

Step-by-Step Gameplay

  1. Select a Boot Amount. Before the round begins, all players must contribute a fixed minimum amount (the 'boot') to the pot. This ensures there is always something to play for.
  2. Deal Three Cards. Each active player receives exactly three cards, dealt face down. Cards are never revealed to other players until the showdown.
  3. Study Your Hand. Players evaluate their cards privately. This is the only stage to decide whether to continue or fold.
  4. Betting Rounds Begin. Players can choose to Call (match the current bet), Raise (increase the bet), or Fold (discard hand and exit the round).
  5. The Showdown. When only one player remains or all active players have matched bets, the remaining players reveal their cards. The best hand wins the pot.

Popular Variations

4 Patti

Similar to Teen Patti but each player receives four cards. The additional card creates more strategic depth and different hand rankings.

Joker Teen Patti

One or more random cards are designated as Jokers before the round. Jokers can substitute for any card to form stronger hands. This variation increases the frequency of premium hands.

AK47

Named after the memorable cards — A, K, 4, and 7 of all suits are declared wild. This creates more action and larger pots due to frequent strong hands.

Betting Terminology

  • Chaal: The act of calling or raising a bet. Players can bet a multiple of the boot amount.
  • Pack: To fold and exit the current round, forfeiting all contributions to the pot.
  • Show: When a player requests to reveal cards before the betting round completes (usually requiring a minimum hand strength).
  • Sideshow: A private comparison between two players, where the weaker hand folds. Less common in formal games.

Responsible Play

Teen Patti is deeply rooted in social gaming culture. Setting personal limits before you start is the single most effective way to keep the game enjoyable. Decide on a budget, stick to it, and never chase losses.
